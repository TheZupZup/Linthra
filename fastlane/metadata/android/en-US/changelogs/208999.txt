Linthra 0.2.8

- Smart pre-cache follows your real queue and never removes songs you downloaded.
- A dropped stream is retried once, then playback moves on to the next track, within limits.
- Large Navidrome/Subsonic libraries sync in batches and resume after an interruption.
- Each artist gets their own Unknown Album.
- Android: FLAC plays on devices without a FLAC decoder.
- Album page: Play next and Add to queue.
