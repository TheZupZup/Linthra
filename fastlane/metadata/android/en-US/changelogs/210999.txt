Linthra 0.2.10

- Faster remote playback starts, skips and track changes.
- Targets Android 17, and asks for local network access when a LAN server needs it.
- Android Auto favorite and shuffle controls, and swipe to queue on Android.
- Sort songs by title, artist, album or date added.
- Many stability fixes for playback, downloads, cache, sync and local libraries.
- Diagnostics no longer include your server's address.
